The acceptance suite in `tests/` is comprehensive, but it belongs to the repo, not the app, and once it's no longer the only guard, a large amount of high-risk transformation logic in `app/src/lib/pokedex.server.ts` and the sorting/URL-state logic in the routes is completely unguarded.

What's unguarded, concretely:

- **Filtering + pagination math** (`pokedex.server.ts:159-168`): combined q/type/generation matching, `Math.min(Math.max(1, ...), pageCount)` clamping and the `(page-1)*48 .. page*48` slice. A refactor that flips a predicate, drops the clamp, or off-by-ones the slice would only be caught by whichever acceptance tests happen to still exercise that exact path.
- **URL state coercion** (`app/src/routes/index.tsx:10-23`): `cleanFilters` stringifies/omits defaults and coerces `page` with `Number()`; NaN, float and string inputs from the query are all hand-rolled.
- **Move-table sorting** (`app/src/routes/pokemon.$name.tsx:159-177`): nulls-last in both directions, ascending-then-descending toggling, tie-break by label, and the `aria-sort` contract.
- **Text transformations** (`pokedex.server.ts:60-76`): "latest" English flavor text (last entry, `\s+` collapse including `\f`), `$effect_chance` substitution, English-name-with-fallback lookups.
- **De-dup and ordering rules** (`pokedex.server.ts:222-252`): the `method:level` dedup key, METHOD_ORDER ranking plus alphabetical tie-break, level-up-by-level ordering.
- **Alternate-form exclusion** (`pokedex.server.ts:20,86,143`): the `id < 10_000` boundary and URL→ID parsing.
- **The API cache** (`pokedex.server.ts:27-48`): 60s TTL, promise-level caching, error eviction, 404 negative caching — a refactor here can silently serve stale or failed responses.

All of this is pure or easily-unit-testable logic that would have cost little to cover; none of it was.
