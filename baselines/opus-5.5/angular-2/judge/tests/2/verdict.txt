The app's own test suite is a single file with three pure-function tests. Everything risky — the ~400-line server-side data builder, home filtering/pagination/URL state, and the move-table sort comparator — is untested. Details below.

## What the app's tests cover

`app/src/app/shared/format.spec.ts:1-20` — three `it` blocks, eight `expect`s, against `humanize`, `dexNumber` and `orDash` in `app/src/app/shared/format.ts:1-20`. These are the safest 20 lines in the codebase: leaf helpers with no branches beyond the trivial. They are, to their credit, well written: exact spec examples (`mr-mime → Mr Mime`), and `orDash(0) === '0'` pins the falsy-zero trap that would render a 0 PP move as `—`.

## What isn't tested — where bugs will hide

**The data layer (`app/src/server/loaders.ts`) — high risk.** This is the app's largest and most bug-prone module and has zero coverage:
- `toCards` at `app/src/server/loaders.ts:159-168` — alternate-form exclusion (`id < 10000`, the off-by-one at ID 10000 is untested), dedup, and the species-name fallback (`species.get(id) ?? ref.name`) that maps `deoxys-normal` → Deoxys. A regression here puts Mega Evolutions in every list, or shows raw Pokémon names instead of species names.
- Move-table assembly at `app/src/server/loaders.ts:265` — the dedupe key `${method}|${move name}|${level-for-level-up}` is exactly the kind of string key someone "simplifies" and then every level-up table grows duplicate rows. Method ranking (287-293) and the selected-game fallback at line 252 (`groups[groups.length-1]`) are equally bare.
- `collapse`/`latestFlavorText`/`shortEffect` at `app/src/server/loaders.ts:126-133` — the spec's whitespace-collapsing rule and the `$effect_chance` substitution. The substitution is only guarded by one acceptance test on one move.
- `loadPageData` at `app/src/server/loaders.ts:391-416` — path routing, the `decodeURIComponent` try/catch (395-397) and the null-for-unknown contract that drives both the 404 and the not-found render.

**Home page filtering/pagination (`app/src/app/pages/home/home.ts`) — high risk.** `matches` at lines 44-53 (three-way filter with humanized-name search), the page clamp at 55-59 (out-of-range `?page=999`, non-numeric `?page=abc`), and `updateFilters`' page reset at 86-90 are all pure-ish logic that could be extracted and tested in seconds. The subtlest thing in the app — the `linkedSignal`/`pushedQ` dance at 40-43 and 73-79 that keeps the search box's text stable while the URL updates through a 200 ms debounce — is exactly the behaviour a refactor breaks silently.

**Move-table sorting (`app/src/app/pages/pokemon/move-table.ts:90-104`) — high risk.** The comparator's null handling (`va === null ? 1 : -1` before the sign is applied, so missing values go last in both directions) plus the tie-break by name is the most intricate logic in the client. One acceptance test sorts one column; missing-value placement under descending sort, and direction flipping, fail nothing.

**Error paths — medium.** Non-404 API failures (`apiGet` throws at `app/src/server/api-client.ts:36-40`) propagate through the Express `/_data` handler's `next(error)` (`app/src/server.ts:23-29`) and through route resolvers (`app/src/app/app.routes.ts:14-22`); nothing verifies a client-side navigation during an API hiccup doesn't wedge the router or that the 404 status via `RESPONSE_INIT` (`app/src/app/pages/not-found/not-found.ts:8-12`) still fires after a refactor.

**Smaller gaps — low/medium.** The TransferState consume-once logic at `app/src/app/data/page-data.ts:56-64` and its 60 s client cache (17-28); `toUrl`'s three accepted URL shapes and the cache-eviction loop at `app/src/server/api-client.ts:60-68`; the title strategy's not-found branch at `app/src/app/page-title-strategy.ts:14-21`; height/weight conversion and stat total at `app/src/app/pages/pokemon/pokemon-page.ts:24-27`; the scroll-restoration state machine at `app/src/app/app.ts:30-46`.

The infrastructure for real tests exists — `@angular/build:unit-test` with vitest is already wired (`app/angular.json:43-44`, `app/tsconfig.spec.json`), and Home/MoveTable logic is trivially extractable or renderable. The agent used it for the one file that needed it least.
