**APIs used and their status:**
- `@Service()` (`app/src/app/data/page-data.ts:48`) — introduced **stable in v22** (June 3, 2026, ~5 months old). Recently-stable, not experimental.
- `linkedSignal` (`app/src/app/pages/home/home.ts:32`, `app/src/app/pages/pokemon/move-table.ts:84`) — stable since v20 (May 2025); the `previous` computation param is also long-standing.

- `AngularNodeAppEngine({allowedHosts})` (`app/src/server.ts:21`) — the `allowedHosts` SSRF-validation option landed Feb/Mar 2026 in stable patch releases; stable, ~8 months old.

- No `unstable_`/`experimental_` names, no experimental build flags in `app/angular.json`.
