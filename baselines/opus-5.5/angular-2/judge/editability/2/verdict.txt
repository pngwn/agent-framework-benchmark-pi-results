The idioms (`@Service`, `linkedSignal`, `input()`, control-flow blocks, vitest) are fully current.

Card ids are assigned in two places (`toCards` in `loaders.ts:124`, `loadHome` in `loaders.ts`), so a data-level fix touches loaders + the `Card` model, but is centralized.
3. **Sort cards by name** — `CardList` covers move/ability/type pages in one edit; the home grid (`home.html:36`) is its own duplicate of the card-grid markup, so the sort (and its interaction with home's pagination in `home.ts:54-69`) needs a second implementation.
4. **Items section** — mostly additive and well-exemplified: new page dir, two routes reusing the `pageData()` resolver helper (`app.routes.ts:9-11`), `loadItems/loadItem` + `ApiItem` + switch cases in `loaders.ts`, two VM interfaces in `models.ts`. `CardList`, `TypeBadge`, `orDash`, `NotFound`, the title strategy and the panel/facts CSS all carry over untouched. Only the ~40-line page template is adapted from `move-page.ts`.
5. **Change data source** — base URL: env read in `api-client.ts:13`, published via TransferState in `app.config.server.ts:17`, consumed via token in `page-data.ts:38-43`, but the `localhost:9000` fallback literal is duplicated (`api-client.ts:13`, `page-data.ts:42`). Caching rules live in two caches (`page-data.ts:14`, `api-client.ts:7`) plus a `Cache-Control` header (`server.ts:25`) — two/three files, each the natural layer.
