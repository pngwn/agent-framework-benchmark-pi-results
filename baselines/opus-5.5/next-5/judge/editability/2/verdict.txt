## Scenario walkthrough

1. **Remove Abilities**: delete `app/src/app/abilities/`; remove the Abilities `<Section>` in `app/src/app/pokemon/[name]/page.tsx:164-170` and one word in `layout.tsx:8`. Nav (`layout.tsx:26-31`) never links to it. Very clean.
2. **Sprite bug**: every card renders through one component — `spriteUrl` at `app/src/components/PokemonCard.tsx:12-13` is the single render site. Caveat: the URL is hand-built from the card's *species id* rather than taken from the API's `front_default`, so a fix that needs per-Pokémon sprite data would require widening `CardData` in two data-shaping sites (`cards.ts:5-23` for ref-based lists, `page.tsx:29-53` + `HomeView.tsx:150-156` for home) — three files in the worst case.
3. **Sort by name everywhere**: card lists split into two paths — `PokemonCardList` (covers moves/abilities/types, one change in `cards.ts:23` or a `sortBy` prop) and `HomeView`'s own slicing (`HomeView.tsx:108`). Two sites.
4. **Items like Moves**: Moves is a single detail route. The reusable parts (`Section`, `PokemonCardList`, `cardsFor`, `api`/`apiOrNull`, format helpers) carry most of it, but the slug-fetch→`notFound`→`generateMetadata` scaffold is copy-pasted per route (`moves/[name]/page.tsx:9-16` mirrors `abilities/[name]/page.tsx:9-16`, `types/[name]/page.tsx:17-24`), so a new resource copies ~10 lines of boilerplate.
5. **API base/caching**: `lib/api.ts` is the only place that knows the base URL (`api.ts:14-16`), TTL/cap (`api.ts:8-9`), fetch mode (`api.ts:38`) and 404 mapping. Wrinkle: `apiBase` is threaded as a prop through 8 files (`PokemonCard.tsx:16`, `HomeView.tsx:41,46`, three pages) to build media URLs client-side, so changing *how* the base is resolved fans out.

Everything else that's shared (`humanize`, `orMissing`, `latestEnglishFlavorText`, `MAX_SPECIES_ID`) lives in one pure module (`format.ts`), which is what keeps the "Deoxys" style fixes single-site.
