## Review: experimentalness

**Verified current stable landscape (Oct 30, 2026):** `hono` 4.13.14 (published 2026-10-25) is the latest stable; hono v5 exists only as `5.0.0-rc.1` (github.com/honojs/hono/releases, npm). `@hono/node-server` v2.0.0 shipped 2026-04-21 with an explicitly unchanged public API, and ^2.1.5 is the current latest (github.com/honojs/node-server/releases). TypeScript 7.0 — the Go-native rewrite — was declared stable 2026-07-08 (devblogs.microsoft.com/typescript/announcing-typescript-7-0). htmx 2.0 has been stable since Oct 2024.

**What the app actually uses:** the entire runtime surface is the long-stable core of Hono 4: `Hono`/`Context`, `c.html/c.header/c.redirect/c.req.param/query`, `app.notFound/onError`, `serveStatic` with `rewriteRequestPath`/`onFound` (`app/src/index.tsx:13,17-27,50-94`), `raw` from `hono/html` (`app/src/index.tsx:3,17`), and `hono/jsx` `FC/Child/PropsWithChildren` (`app/src/components.tsx:1-13`), including async components (`app/src/pages/home.tsx:70`, `app/src/pages/pokemon.tsx:46,171`) — all stable since hono 4.0. htmx usage (`hx-get/trigger/swap/sync/target`, `HX-Push-Url`, `HX-History-Restore-Request`, `app/src/index.tsx:16,56-58,72`) is core, non-experimental htmx 1.x/2.x API. Node-side code uses only stable globals (`fetch`, `import.meta.dirname`, `fs.cpSync`, `createRequire` — `app/src/api.ts:33`, `app/src/index.tsx:25`, `app/scripts/copy-assets.mjs:8-13`). No `unstable_`/`experimental_` names, no experimental flags in `app/tsconfig.json`.

**Recently-stable (not experimental, excluded from count):** TypeScript 7.0.2 as a devDependency/build tool (`app/package.json:15`) — stable for ~4 months, semantically identical to TS 6; and `@hono/node-server` ^2.1.5 — the v2 major is ~6 months old but API-identical to v1. The app deliberately stays on hono 4.x rather than the v5 RC — the correct, low-risk call.
