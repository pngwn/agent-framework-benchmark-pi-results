**What's there.** The measured facts report zero `experimental_`/`unstable_` names and no config flags, and a grep of `app/src` confirms it — no experimental, preview, RC, or canary API appears anywhere. What the app *does* do is ride the current stable stack at its very newest majors:

- **Supporting majors at the current line**: Vite 8.3, `vite-plugin-svelte` 7.2, `adapter-node` 6, TypeScript 6, Node ≥ 22.17 (`app/package.json:4-16`) — exactly what Kit 3 requires per its changelog ("require @sveltejs/vite-plugin-svelte v7", "TypeScript 6 is now the minimum required version").
