- **Data loading** is entirely `routeLoader$` with URL-driven state (`routes/index.tsx:13`, `routes/pokemon/[name]/index.tsx:16`, `routes/team/index.tsx:9`), `useNavigate` for filter/game changes, `resolveValue` for `DocumentHead`, catchall 404 route — all per the current routing/route-loader docs.
- **State**: `useSignal`/`useTask$`/`isBrowser` from `@builder.io/qwik/build`; the team store is a hand-rolled cookie helper, but Qwik City has no client cookie API (the open feature request #2927 confirms this), and the cookie choice is what makes the SSR-correct first paint the FEATURE requires.
