The codebase is idiomatic and well-layered: all API access lives in `app/src/lib/server/api.ts`, page data shaping in `pokedex.ts`, presentational components take narrow DTOs from `types.ts`, and every route follows the same thin `+page.server.ts` → `error(404)` → `+page.svelte` pattern. The main editability cost is that "build a Card for a species" is implemented three separate ways inside `pokedex.ts`, and team mutation logic is duplicated across two components.
