Reviewing as a PR against Qwik 1.20's current tooling: the framework ships first-class unit-test support (`@builder.io/qwik/testing` + Vitest, scaffolded by `qwik add vitest`), and this app has **none of it** — no vitest config, no test script in `app/package.json`, zero test files (confirmed by static facts and by `grep` for `describe/it/test/expect` across `app/`). The app's entire safety net is the acceptance suite in `tests/` (layout, home, pokemon, details specs), which the agent was handed.

That's a real loss here, because the app's riskiest logic is *pure or near-pure* and would be cheap to pin down: the sort comparator in `moves-table.tsx`, the URL-state builder in `urls.ts`, the filter/paginate math in `routes/index.tsx`, and the moves grouping/version-group selection in the Pokémon route. Instead, all of it is only guarded by end-to-end assertions that check one or two happy-path fixtures, so a refactor of the data layer could break edge cases (nulls-last sorting in both directions, the `page` clamp, alternate-form exclusion, the default-game = highest `order` rule) without anything failing.
