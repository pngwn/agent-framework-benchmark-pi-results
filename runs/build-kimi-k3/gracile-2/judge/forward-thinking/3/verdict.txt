**What I verified against current docs/templates:**
- `app/server.js` is essentially verbatim the official `minimal-server-express` starter's `server.js` (`nodeAdapter`, `getClientBuildPath`, `printUrls`, `express.static`), minus the demo middleware.
- `app/vite.config.js` matches the official template's `gracile({ output: 'server' })`.
- `defineRoute` handler/`document`/`template` shape, `context.props.GET`, `context.responseInit.status`, the `*.client.js` per-route client entrypoint convention, `<route-template-outlet>`, and `html` from `@gracile/gracile/server-html` all match the annotated examples on gracile.js.org — no legacy or deprecated Gracile APIs found. (The client-side router `@gracile-labs/client-router` is documented as experimental, so not using it is defensible.)
- Deps: `lit` is a required peer of `@gracile/engine` (confirmed in `app/package-lock.json:498-503`), so it's necessary despite zero direct Lit imports.
