## Review summary

The app is a clean, idiomatic TanStack Start codebase: file-based routes, `createServerFn` wrappers in `lib/queries.ts` with server-only helpers in `lib/data.server.ts` (matching the framework's documented `.functions.ts` / `.server.ts` split), thin route components, and presentational shared components (`PokemonCardList`, `MoveTable`, `Page`). Verified against the current docs, the server-function usage (`{ method: 'GET' }`, `.validator((data) => data)`, `.handler`, called as `loadXPage({ data })` in loaders) is current, not legacy.

The editability tax concentrates in one place: `lib/data.server.ts` is a ~780-line module holding every API record type, the fetch/cache layer, and all six page-data builders. Three of those builders are near copy-paste of each other (Pokémon-ref → card list construction appears at data.server.ts:624-640, 672-686 and 742-756), so anything touching cards or ordering lands in 3–4 spots in that one file. Smaller duplication: `head()` title logic repeated across four route files, and the home page encodes its search-params shape three times (`validateSearch`, `update`, `linkSearch`).

Removal and data-source changes are cheap; behavior changes to cards and new "shaped like Moves" features mean appending to and editing the monolith.
