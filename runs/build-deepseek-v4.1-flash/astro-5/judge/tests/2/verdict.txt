The risky logic that is completely untested is substantial:

- **404 handling** — `isNotFound` catch + `Astro.response.status = 404` per route (`pokemon/[name].astro:18-33`, `types/[name].astro:19-30`), non-404 errors rethrown (unrendered 500).
