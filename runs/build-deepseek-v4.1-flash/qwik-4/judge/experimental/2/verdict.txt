All exploration done. Summary of what I found:

The app is essentially a stock Qwik City scaffold (Qwik 1.20.3, the current stable 1.x line) with Pokédex routes added. Every framework API it touches — `component$`, `useSignal`, `useComputed$`, `routeLoader$`, `useLocation`, `useDocumentHead`, `DocumentHead`, `QwikCityProvider`, `RouterOutlet`, `Slot`, `isDev`, `createQwikCity` node middleware, `qwikVite`/`qwikCity` plugins — has been stable in Qwik/Qwik City since the 1.x era (≥1.2, early 2024; most since 1.0, June 2023). There are no `experimental_`/`unstable_` names, no canary/RC packages, and no opt-in preview flags. The one measured "experimental" marker, `vite.config.ts:55 preview: {`, is a false positive: `preview` is Vite's long-stable preview-options config (used here exactly as the Qwik starter template does, to serve the SSR build via `vite preview` + `@builder.io/qwik-city/middleware/node`). Notably, Qwik 2.0.0-rc.4 shipped in late October 2026 and the app deliberately stays on the stable 1.20.3 line — the conservative choice. Only cruft: an unused `"undici": "*"` devDependency.
