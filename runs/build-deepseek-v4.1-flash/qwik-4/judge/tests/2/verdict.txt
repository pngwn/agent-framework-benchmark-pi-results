What makes this costly is that `app/src/lib/data.ts` and the route loaders carry nearly all of the spec's trap-laden logic: alternate-form exclusion (`id >= 10000`, `data.ts:283`), Pokémon→species mapping with dedup and hidden-ability merging (`data.ts:271-292`), missing-values-last sorting (`pokemon/[name]/index.tsx:207-225`), humanization and English-name fallbacks (`data.ts:117-136`), filter/pagination math (`index.tsx:44-64`), and URL-query parsing (`data.ts:320-332`).
