## Review summary

The app sits entirely on the **current stable stack** (SvelteKit 3.1 / Svelte 5.57 / Vite 8.3 / adapter-node 6, per `app/package-lock.json:394`) and reaches for **zero experimental, preview, RC or canary APIs**: no `experimental.remoteFunctions`, no `compilerOptions.experimental.async`, no `unstable_*` anywhere — matching the measured `"experimental": []`. Svelte-level usage (`$props`/`$state`/`$derived` at `app/src/routes/+layout.svelte:6`, `untrack` at `app/src/routes/+page.svelte:8`, recursive `{#snippet}`/`{@render}` at `app/src/routes/pokemon/[name]/+page.svelte:24-35`) has been stable since Svelte 5.0 (Nov 2024). `$app/state#page` (`app/src/routes/pokemon/[name]/+page.svelte:3`) is the *only* option left in Kit 3 (`$app/stores` is removed). `error(404, 'Not found')` (`app/src/routes/pokemon/[name]/+page.server.js:68`) is the currently recommended `(status, message)` form.

The only "new-ish" surface is the newly-stable framework itself, which is mostly forced: SvelteKit 3 (stable 2026-10-01, per svelte.dev/blog/sveltekit-3-is-here) mandates config-in-`vite.config.js` and `#lib` subpath imports (`app/vite.config.js:5-13`, `app/package.json:22-25`); Vite 8 and Svelte 5.57 are the minimums Kit 3 requires. The one optional modern touch is the parametric `runes: ({ filename }) => ...` compile option (`app/vite.config.js:6-9`), a stable compiler feature shipped ~March 2026 (sveltejs/svelte PR #17951, commit aaf405b) used to force runes mode for first-party code — justified, honest ("Can be removed in svelte 6"), and isolated to one config block.

The notable defect runs in the *opposite* direction of experimentalness — it's a half-done migration to Kit 3: `goto(url, { keepFocus: true, noScroll: true, replaceState: true })` at `app/src/routes/+page.svelte:34-37` and `app/src/routes/pokemon/[name]/+page.svelte:12-15`. Kit 3 removed `noScroll`/`keepFocus` in favor of `reset` (PR #16558; error `goto_options_removed` at svelte.dev/e/kit/goto_options_removed — the guard throws in dev but the stale options are silently ignored in production, so scroll/focus reset on every debounced search commit), and `replaceState` is deprecated in favor of `replace` (still honored via the `opts.replace ?? opts.replaceState` shim in Kit's runtime).
