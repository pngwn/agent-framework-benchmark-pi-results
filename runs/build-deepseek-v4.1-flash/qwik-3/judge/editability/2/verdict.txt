I've reviewed the app against the spec and current Qwik City practices. Here's my assessment.

## Scenario walkthrough

1. **Remove Abilities** — delete `app/src/routes/abilities/`, but then `getAbilityPage`, the `hiddenFor` callback in `cardsFromPokemonRefs`, `PokemonCard.hidden` and the hidden-ability span in `ui.tsx`, plus the Abilities section in the Pokémon page all become dead code. 4 files, scattered but findable.
2. **Sprite bug (Deoxys)** — every non-home card list flows through one function, `cardsFromPokemonRefs` (`api.ts:193-215`), which is the single place that maps a Pokémon ref → id → `spriteUrl(id)`. Home cards are built separately from species ids (`api.ts:187`), so one fix in that function covers the learned-by/ability/type lists; home needs a glance but is already species-id-based.
3. **Sort by name** — either two edits in `api.ts` (`cardsFromPokemonRefs` sort at line 215, `loadHomeIndex` card build) or two card components; no flag soup.
4. **Items section** — route scaffolding, loader pattern and `PokemonCards`/`NotFound`/`displayNumber`/panel CSS reuse, but the Moves page's summary `<dl>` and section markup are inline in the route file, so the body is copied; a new `getItemPage` gets appended to the already-460-line `api.ts`.
5. **Data source / caching** — `API_BASE` (`api.ts:5`), `spriteUrl` (`api.ts:60`), `apiUrl` (`api.ts:64-68`) and both caches (`api.ts:6`, `api.ts:303`) all live in one file. Trivial.

The one non-idiomatic tangle: `routes/index.tsx` drives the URL with raw `window.history.pushState/replaceState` in five inline handlers plus a `useVisibleTask$` `popstate` re-sync task, bypassing Qwik City's `useNavigate()`/`useLocation()` reactivity, which is why the manual sync exists at all.
